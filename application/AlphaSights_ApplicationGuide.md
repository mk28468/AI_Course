## INTERVIEW PREPARATION

**If invited to interview, prepare stories around:**

1. **Client-expert matching:** Tell the story of launching the two AI minors — mapping what industry needs, what students need, brokering partnerships, managing conflicting priorities.

2. **Fast-paced multitasking:** Walk through a typical semester managing 7 programs, 50 faculty, 400 students, and responding to Registrar/Dean requests in real-time.

3. **Negotiation & persuasion:** Give an example of convincing faculty to teach a new course, or persuading a company to sponsor a student project, or resolving a scheduling conflict between competing interests.

4. **AI + business intersection:** Explain how you integrated LLMs and AI tools into curriculum, and what you learned about how organizations adopt new technologies.

**Practice the STAR format:**
- **Situation:** Here's the multi-stakeholder constraint I was managing
- **Task:** Here's what success looked like (deadline, outcome, stakeholder satisfaction)
- **Action:** Here's what I did (negotiation, analysis, coordination, communication)
- **Result:** Here's what happened (program launched, deadline met, all parties satisfied, specific metric)
